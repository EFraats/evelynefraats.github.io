🐱 The Cat That Makes the Brain Better

Interactive neuroscience workshop for children (10-14y)

Available throughout Belgium & the Netherlands

About the Workshop

In this workshop, children will discover how doctors and scientists can influence the brain. They will explore how certain parts of the brain can become more active or, instead, calmer, and learn how these approaches may help people recover from illness. The ultimate goal is to inspire children to marvel at just how extraordinary the human brain really is. Children will learn about different regions of the brain and explore innovative techniques used to influence them. Guided by Evelyne Fraats, a PhD student and brain researcher, and her cat Mittens, participants are taken on a fun and interactive storytelling journey into the fascinating world of neuroscience. Through creative activities and hands-on experiments, children will experience for themselves how the brain works and how scientists can influence it to help people feel a little better. They will investigate fascinating questions, test ideas, and discover just how powerful, adaptable, and remarkable the human brain can be. Along the way, they will step into the role of young brain researchers, learning about science through exploration, curiosity, and play.Through creative activities and hands-on experiments, you will experience for yourself how the brain works and how we can influence it to help people feel a little better. We will explore fascinating questions, test ideas, and discover just how powerful and adaptable the human brain can be.

What Children Learn

  • 🧠 What is the brain?
  • 🗺️ What are the different brain areas, and what roles do they play?
  • 🩺 How is the brain related to certain mental health disorders, such as depression?
  • ⚡ What is brain stimulation, and how can it influence the brain?
  • 🌟 How can brain stimulation help people with certain mental health disorders?

Available For

  • Primary schools
  • High schools
  • Science festivals
  • Other public outreach engagement activities

📍 Practical Information

The workshop is available for schools, science festivals, and other public outreach engagement activities across Belgium.

The content can be adapted to different ages, educational levels, and audiences. The original workshop is designed for children between 10 and 14y.
