The cat that makes the brain better

Interactive neuroscience workshop (EN/NL) for children (10-14 years)

Developed by Evelyne Fraats, Doctoral Researcher in Social Neuroscience

About the workshop

In this workshop, children will discover how doctors and scientists can influence the brain. The ultimate goal is to inspire children about the human brain and how we can change the brain to make people feel a "little better".

Children will learn about different regions of the brain and explore innovative techniques used to influence them. Guided by myself, Evelyne Fraats, a doctoral researcher, and my cat Mittens, participants are taken on a fun and interactive storytelling journey into the fascinating world of neuroscience.

The workshop makes use of storytelling, creative painting activities, and hands-on experiments.

What Children Learn

  • 🧠 What is the brain?
  • 🗺️ What are the different brain areas, and what roles do they play?
  • 🩺 How is the brain related to certain mental health disorders, such as depression?
  • ⚡ What is brain stimulation, and how can it influence the brain?
  • 🌟 How can brain stimulation help people with certain mental health disorders?

Practical information

The workshop is available for schools, science festivals, and other public outreach activities across Belgium and the Netherlands.

The content can be adapted to different ages, educational levels, and audiences. The original workshop is designed for children aged 10-14 years.
