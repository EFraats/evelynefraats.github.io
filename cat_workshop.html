(EN) The Cat That Makes the Brain Better

(NL) De kat die het brein beter maakt

Interactive neuroscience workshop for children (10-14y)

Developed by Evelyne Fraats, Doctoral researcher in Social Neuroscience

About the Workshop

In this workshop, children will discover how doctors and scientists can influence the brain. The ultimate goal is to inspire children about the human brain and how we can change the brain to make people feel a "bit better". Children will learn about different regions of the brain and explore innovative techniques used to influence them. Guided by myself, Evelyne Fraats, a doctoral researcher, and my cat Mittens, participants are taken on a fun and interactive storytelling journey into the fascinating world of neuroscience. The workshop makes use of storytelling, creative painting activities and hands-on experiments for the children.

What Children Learn

  • 🧠 What is the brain?
  • 🗺️ What are the different brain areas, and what roles do they play?
  • 🩺 How is the brain related to certain mental health disorders, such as depression?
  • ⚡ What is brain stimulation, and how can it influence the brain?
  • 🌟 How can brain stimulation help people with certain mental health disorders?

📍 Practical Information

The workshop is available for schools, science festivals, and other public outreach engagement activities across Belgium and the Netherlands.

The content can be adapted to different ages, educational levels, and audiences. The original workshop is designed for children between 10 and 14 years old.

The workshop is available in Dutch and English

A small expense allowance is requested to cover travel costs, workshop materials, and preparation time. The exact amount is open for discussion and can be adjusted to the organization.
