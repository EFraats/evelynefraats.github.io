Interactive neuroscience workshop for children

Available throughout Belgium

About the Workshop

The Cat That Makes the Brain Better is an interactive science communication workshop designed to introduce children to neuroscience in an engaging and accessible way. Through storytelling, discussion and playful activities, children learn how the brain helps us think, feel, decide and learn.

What Children Learn

  • 🧠 What does the brain do?
  • 🐱 How do we make decisions?
  • ⚡ How do scientists study the brain?
  • 🎲 What is an experiment?
  • 💡 Why do people think differently?

Available For

  • Primary schools
  • Libraries
  • Science festivals
  • Family events
  • Public engagement events
