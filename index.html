Cognitive Neuroscience PhD Researcher

I am a PhD researcher interested in how people experience control over their actions and decisions. My work combines behavioural methods, brain stimulation, computational approaches, and cognitive neuroscience to understand the mechanisms underlying the sense of agency.

About Me

Welcome! My research explores how humans predict, monitor, and experience their own actions. I am passionate about bridging fundamental neuroscience with real-world applications and public engagement.

Research

Sense of Agency

Understanding why we feel responsible for our actions and decisions.

Predictive Processing

Investigating how predictions shape perception, action, and cognition.

Brain Stimulation

Using non-invasive brain stimulation to study causal neural mechanisms.

Human Behaviour

Examining cognitive control, decision-making, and human performance.
