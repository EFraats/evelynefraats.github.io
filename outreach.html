🐱 The Cat That Makes the Brain Better

An interactive workshop developed for children that introduces basic neuroscience concepts through storytelling, games, and hands-on activities.

Children learn how the brain works, how we make decisions, and how scientists study the mind while following the adventures of a curious cat.
